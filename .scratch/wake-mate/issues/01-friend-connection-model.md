## Question

Should Wake Mate require mutual acceptance for a Friend Connection (both people confirm, like Facebook friends), or is a one-directional model acceptable (like following), where A can send Alarm Calls to B without B explicitly accepting first?

## Answer

Mutual acceptance required. Both users must confirm before either can Share Alarm Calls with the other. Chosen to match the brief's "friend connections" language and to avoid unsolicited-audio abuse given there's no content moderation in the MVP.
