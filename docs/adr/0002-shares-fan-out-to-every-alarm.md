# Shares fan out to every current Alarm, not one designated receiving Alarm

When a Friend Connection Shares an Alarm Call, ticket 10 enqueues it into *every* one of the recipient's currently-existing Alarms independently, rather than a single designated "receiving" Alarm the user picks in settings. A user with two Alarms (e.g. a weekday and a weekend one) can therefore have the same clip queued — and eventually played — on both, rather than exactly once. This was chosen over a single-receiving-Alarm design specifically to avoid building an undesigned "which Alarm receives Shares" settings surface within Wake Mate's 4-week solo-dev MVP timeline; the double-play quirk was accepted as cheaper than that missing UI. Alarms created after a Share was already queued do not retroactively receive it.
